# Sustainability Checklist Consolidation Design

**Date:** 2026-08-10

## Goal

Retire the standalone **Sustainability Checklist** SOP. Move every check from
the supplied Daily Sustainability Checklist into the existing **Morning
Checklist** and **Night Checklist**, preserving each check as a separately
auditable item.

## Existing records

The three current SOP templates are active and each has three active
assignments:

- Morning Checklist (28 existing items)
- Night Checklist (29 existing items)
- Sustainability Checklist (22 existing items)

The current Sustainability Checklist and its records will be deleted after the
destination templates have been updated. This deliberately deletes its three
assignments, five completion records, and one item-level completion record.

## Structure

The existing Morning and Night items remain unchanged. New items are placed in
named sections on each template so sustainability evidence is distinguishable
from broader operational checks. Each section follows the source document's
area headings.

The source calls the second shift **Evening**; the application uses **Night**.

## Item mapping

### Morning Checklist

#### Sustainability — Energy

- Electricity meter reading recorded (8 AM)
- Generator hours/fuel checked (if used) (10 AM)
- Unnecessary lights/equipment switched off
- AC settings within standard (22°C) (on arrival)

#### Sustainability — Water

- Water meter reading recorded (8 AM)
- Leaks checked in guest/staff/public areas
- No unnecessary water wastage observed

#### Sustainability — Waste

- Waste segregated correctly
- Waste collection area clean
- Hazardous waste stored separately
- No waste piling (not collected)

#### Sustainability — Chemicals

- PPE available and being used

#### Sustainability — Grounds & Biodiversity

- No rat poison or other out
- No wildlife disturbance observed
- Checked for invasive plants; record any observance

#### Sustainability — Guest Engagement

- Sustainability information displayed
- Towel/linen reuse programme active (mainly in the morning)

#### Sustainability — Pest Management

- Ask cleaning and kitchen staff whether they noticed pest activity (for example, droppings)
- Record observations and remediation strategies
- Confirm past remediation strategies were implemented and record their effectiveness
- No rat poison placed (use rat cages/traps instead)

#### Sustainability — General

- Any other environmental concerns reported
- Corrective actions assigned

### Night Checklist

#### Sustainability — Energy

- Electricity meter reading recorded (5:30 PM)
- Unnecessary lights/equipment switched off
- AC settings within standard (22°C) (on arrival)
- Fuel logs updated (if vehicles are there)
- Gas usage recorded

#### Sustainability — Waste

- Waste collection area clean

#### Sustainability — Chemicals

- Chemical store clean and ventilated
- Chemical containers labelled
- PPE available and being used
- MSDS displayed correctly as per styling guide

#### Sustainability — Guest Engagement

- Towel/linen reuse programme active (mainly in the morning)

#### Sustainability — Pest Management

- No rat poison placed (use rat cages/traps instead)

## Shared checks

The following seven checks appear in both checklists, exactly as indicated by
the source document:

- Electricity meter reading recorded
- Unnecessary lights/equipment switched off
- AC settings within standard (22°C)
- Waste collection area clean
- PPE available and being used
- Towel/linen reuse programme active
- No rat poison placed (use rat cages/traps instead)

They are duplicated as independent checklist items because the Morning and
Night SOPs are completed independently.

## Data changes

1. Add the listed sections and items to the Morning Checklist.
2. Add the listed sections and items to the Night Checklist.
3. Delete the standalone Sustainability Checklist. Database cascades remove its
   assignments, completions, and item completions.

The destination templates already have active assignments, so their assignees
will receive the consolidated content through their existing SOP assignments.
